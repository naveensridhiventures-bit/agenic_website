The company behind every pack.

Agenic Agritech India Private Limited is a Chennai-based FMCG company on a mission to make quality everyday products trusted, traceable and accessible.

Our story

Built for the Indian kitchen and beyond.

Agenic was formed to bring a modern, brand-led approach to everyday food and household needs. We work with licensed manufacturing partners, set clear quality standards, and make every pack traceable with a QR code — so customers and trade partners always know what they are buying and who made it.

We begin with fresh foods and are expanding into a broader portfolio of daily-need categories, serving households, retail stores, supermarkets, hotels and institutional caterers across Chennai and beyond.
